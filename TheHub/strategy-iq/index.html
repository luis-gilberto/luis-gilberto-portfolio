COMMON QUESTIONS

How long does it take?
The assessment takes about 10-15 minutes to complete. It requires you to think critically about your business, so don't rush it.
What do I receive at the end?
You get immediate access to your Maturity Score dashboard and a downloadable Strategic Brief PDF outlining gaps and steps.
Is this a sales call?
No. StrategyIQ™ is a consultant-led diagnostic session. We run your brand through the intelligence engine together to identify gaps and opportunities in real-time. You leave with a roadmap, not a pitch.

Execute the brief with your team, or use the "Request Advisory" link to review results with Luis directly.
