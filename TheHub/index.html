How It Works

What actually happens when you engage.

No ambiguity. No mystery retainer. Here is the exact sequence from first contact to active engagement.

1
Submit the Intake

A short form that captures your organization context, the problem you are trying to solve, your timeline, and your current constraints. It takes about five minutes.

This does two things immediately: it filters whether the engagement is a fit, and it feeds directly into the StrategyIQ diagnostic.

Start the intake
2
StrategyIQ Runs the Diagnostic

Your intake is processed through StrategyIQ. The output is a structured read of your situation — positioning gaps, growth bottlenecks, messaging misalignment, operational friction.

This becomes the basis for the strategy conversation. Not a generic slide deck — a diagnostic built from what you actually submitted.

Powered by StrategyIQ
3
Book the Strategy Call

You book a working session. On that call we review the diagnostic together, clarify the key constraints and opportunities, and determine the scope of the engagement.

This is where the problem moves from assessment to action plan.

Live session
4
Access The Portal Workspace

Once the engagement begins, you get access to The Portal. Strategy documents, deliverables, messaging frameworks, campaign plans — everything lives in one structured workspace.

No more work scattered across email and decks. The Portal is where the engagement actually runs.

Via The Portal
5
The Hub Runs Behind It All

You interact with The Portal. Behind the scenes, The Hub is the operating model connecting the diagnostic, the workspace, and the engagement framework.

The Hub is the system. The Portal is what you see. StrategyIQ is what makes it think.

Structured process · a clear sequence from intake to active engagement. Diagnostic insight · a StrategyIQ read of your situation before any work begins. Live workspace · a dedicated Portal environment where everything stays organized. Clear deliverables · strategy documents, frameworks, and plans. Not just advice.

All of this happens inside The Hub · the strategic workspace where diagnostics, strategy, and delivery connect into a single operating system.
