What this means in practice

  • The Hub This is where the system lives. It holds the strategy, frameworks, and operating model so everything stays connected.
  • Advisory This is the strategic leadership layer. It is where direction gets shaped and decisions are clarified before work begins.
  • The Studio This is where the work gets built. Campaigns, design, messaging, and creative execution happen here.
  • The Portal This is the operating environment. It keeps projects organized and execution moving once strategy is defined.
  • StrategyIQ This is the intelligence layer. It analyzes inputs, surfaces insights, and helps the system turn complex problems into clear strategic paths.
