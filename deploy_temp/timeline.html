MY Journey

From Venezuela to Seattle. From scholarship student to strategic marketing leader. From mobile entertainment pioneer to AI-powered consultant.

This is the complete story of how curiosity, resilience, and a refusal to be pigeonholed shaped 25 years of creative and strategic work.
