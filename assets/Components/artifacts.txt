          <p class="finding-head">Why the audience responds the way it does.</p>
          <p class="finding-body">Parents arrive during moments of crisis — not to learn, but to survive the next 10 minutes. Content that names invisible cycles goes viral because it de-shames, decodes, and gives forward momentum all at once.</p>

          <p class="finding-body">Most parenting creators occupy aspirational territory. Criar Sin Culpas occupies the opposite: the moment everything has already broken down. The combination of lived experience, PCIT-grounded tools, and non-shaming honesty creates a positioning that cannot be copied by credential or warmth alone.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="finding" style="border-color:#D4933A;">
          <p class="finding-head">What category she is actually occupying.</p>
          <p class="finding-body">Not "parenting advice." The actual category is: <strong style="color:#F5F0EB;">crisis-moment intervention for Spanish-speaking families navigating escalation and dysregulation.</strong> This category has no dominant voice. First-mover positioning here is the real strategic prize.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

        <p class="sec-label">Why Translation Is the Right Frame</p>
        <div class="data-rows">
          <div class="data-row"><span class="data-key">The Asset</span><span class="data-val">50+ workshops and sessions of real parenting intervention — not theory, lived practice.</span></div>
          <div class="data-row"><span class="data-key">The Gap</span><span class="data-val">That knowledge exists as isolated events, not as a navigable system parents can enter and progress through.</span></div>
          <div class="data-row"><span class="data-key">The Implication</span><span class="data-val">The first offer should not be new content. It should be a structured entry point into content and expertise that already exists.</span></div>
        </div>
