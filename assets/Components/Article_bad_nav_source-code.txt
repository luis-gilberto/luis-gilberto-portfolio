        <main class="article-content">
            <div class="content-section">
                <h2 class="section-title">The Challenge</h2>
                <p class="section-text">In Spring 2021, Microsoft Teams was synonymous with workplace collaboration—Zoom calls, work meetings, corporate productivity. The challenge was repositioning it as a consumer platform for families and friends without alienating the B2B base that made Teams successful. How do you make a "work app" feel essential for personal life?</p>
            </div>

            <div class="content-section">
                <h2 class="section-title">Strategic Approach</h2>
                <p class="section-text">As Strategic Architect and Narrative Strategy Lead, I developed the "Action + Together" messaging framework—showing Teams enabled people to DO things together, not just talk. This wasn't about features; it was about reframing what "together" means in a post-pandemic world.</p>
                
                <ul class="feature-list">
                    <li class="feature-item">
                        <div class="feature-title">"Action + Together" Framework</div>
                        <div class="feature-description">Created messaging architecture positioning Teams as platform for shared experiences—watching movies together, planning events together, celebrating milestones together—not just video calls</div>
                    </li>
                    <li class="feature-item">
                        <div class="feature-title">Modular Bill of Materials (BoM)</div>
                        <div class="feature-description">Built scalable campaign system ensuring cross-platform consistency—from hero films to social content to in-app onboarding—all reinforcing core "Action + Together" narrative</div>
                    </li>
                    <li class="feature-item">
                        <div class="feature-title">Custom 3D Emoji System</div>
                        <div class="feature-description">Developed playful visual language through custom 3D emojis that made Teams feel warm and personal, breaking away from corporate aesthetic</div>
                    </li>
                    <li class="feature-item">
                        <div class="feature-title">Together Mode Innovation</div>
                        <div class="feature-description">Showcased Together Mode as signature feature making virtual gatherings feel more connected—positioning technology as enabler of human connection, not replacement</div>
                    </li>
                </ul>
            </div>

            <div class="content-section">
                <h2 class="section-title">Impact & Results</h2>
                <p class="section-text">The consumer launch exceeded expectations across every metric. 568M impressions generated massive awareness, while 1.8M downloads in launch month proved the repositioning resonated. Brand awareness increased 340% among consumer audiences, and sentiment improved 92%—proving Teams successfully shed its "corporate tool" perception.</p>
                
                <p class="section-text">The modular BoM approach enabled rapid deployment across markets while maintaining narrative consistency, and the "Action + Together" framework became the foundation for Teams' ongoing consumer marketing.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="content-section">
                <h2 class="section-title">Key Learnings</h2>
                <p class="section-text">This launch reinforced that successful repositioning requires more than new messaging—it demands rethinking every touchpoint. By focusing on shared experiences rather than features, we transformed how consumers perceived Teams. The modular approach proved essential for maintaining consistency while adapting to platform-specific needs.</p>
            </div>
