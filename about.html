Three Ways of Seeing

01

The Systems Thinker

I didn't set out to be a strategist. I set out to make sense of things. Syntax, stories, systems: all languages waiting to be decoded.

Born in Venezuela, I landed in the U.S. with a scholarship and a stubborn belief: building from scratch is a feature, not a flaw.

When Copilot in Edge was still a whisper, I helped shape early campaigns. Digging into use cases, decoding nascent technology, crafting stories that made people open Edge for something they'd never heard of. It wasn't just marketing; it was translation, persuasion, and a little bit of faith

Systematic Intelligence

I don't rely on intuition alone. Every campaign, every brand story, every launch is built on frameworks that turn complexity into clarity. From ScopeIQ to StrategyIQ, I've productized 15+ years of hard-won experience into systems that deliver.

02

The Craftsman

Attention Architecture

My first job was as a magician. I learned how to hold attention and misdirect with grace. That instinct never left.

Startups taught me speed and scrappiness. Microsoft gave me plot twists: global storytelling, high-stakes launches, and the kind of creative pressure that forces clarity.

I've shaped narratives for Surface, Office, and Teams. But the through-line has always been the same: craft that respects the audience's intelligence.

This site isn't just a portfolio; it's a proof-of-life. A stitched-together map of the work, people, and philosophies that shaped me. If it feels personal, that's because it is.
