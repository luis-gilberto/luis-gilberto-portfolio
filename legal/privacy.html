Privacy Policy

This policy describes what information may be collected through luis-gilberto.com and how it is handled.

Last updated · August 12, 2026

  • Personal information is not sold.
  • Information you choose to provide is used to respond to you or schedule a conversation.
  • Limited analytics may be used to understand aggregate site usage.
01

Information this site may collect

When you visit luis-gilberto.com, limited technical and usage information may be processed in the ordinary course of operating the site.

This can include aggregate page-view information from privacy-oriented analytics, as well as standard technical data that hosting and content-delivery services may process to serve pages reliably (for example, request timing or browser-related technical signals).

The site also uses browser storage for preferences such as theme or language settings on certain pages. That information stays in your browser unless you clear it.

02

Information you choose to provide

You may choose to share information when you:

  • send a message through the contact form
  • email hello@luis-gilberto.com
  • request a conversation through Microsoft Bookings

Depending on the channel, this may include your name, email address, company, project details, and any other information you decide to include.

Some interactive Studio experiences may keep short text you enter in your browser session only. That content is not submitted as part of the contact form unless you separately choose to share it.

03

How information is used

Information is used for practical purposes related to this site and professional conversations, including:

  • responding to inquiries
  • scheduling conversations
  • understanding aggregate site usage
  • maintaining and improving site performance

It is not used to build advertising profiles for sale, and it is not used for third-party advertising on this site.

04

Third-party services

Parts of the site rely on third-party processors to operate. The services currently used in active site experiences include:

  • Plausible Analytics — privacy-oriented, aggregate website analytics
  • Formspree — processing of contact-form submissions
  • Microsoft Bookings — scheduling conversations when you choose that path
  • Hosting and delivery services — serving the site and related assets (including CDN-hosted fonts, icons, and media where used)

When you use a third-party service, that provider’s own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

05

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Plausible Analytics to understand aggregate traffic. Plausible is configured as a privacy-oriented analytics service and is not used here as an advertising tracker.

Some pages also use local browser storage for interface preferences (for example, light/dark theme or language). Third-party fonts, media hosts, or booking tools may set their own cookies or similar technologies when those services load.

No advertising pixels or ad-network trackers were identified as part of the active luis-gilberto.com front-end audited for this policy.

06

Selling personal information

Personal information is not sold.

Information may be shared with the processors named above only as needed to operate the site, respond to you, or schedule a conversation.

07

Retention and control

Contact and booking information is kept only as long as needed to respond, maintain a professional record of the conversation, or meet ordinary business obligations.

You can ask questions about information you have provided, or request correction or deletion where appropriate, by contacting the address below. Browser preferences stored locally can usually be cleared through your browser settings.

This policy does not claim a fixed retention calendar for every data type, because retention depends on the nature of the inquiry and ordinary business needs.

08

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@luis-gilberto.com or use the contact page.

09

Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site or its services change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when the policy is substantively revised.
