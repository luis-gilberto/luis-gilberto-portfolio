The throughline

Strategy is not a title. It is a pattern of becoming.

Every chapter added a layer: technical fluency, startup resourcefulness, Microsoft-scale product marketing, human-centered storytelling, and now AI-enabled operating systems.

The result is not a traditional portfolio. It is a body of proof.
