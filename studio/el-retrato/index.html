The premise

It does not begin
with a solution.
It begins with a read.

A traditional brief describes the task. A read explains the person doing it. El Retrato collects your words, references, and images, and returns a clear starting point built from evidence.

Two ways to use it

01

Read your own practice.

Stop in front of your work and see it clearly before deciding what to build next.

02

Open one with a client.

Understand the commission before answering it. Lead from clarity, not assumption.

Portrait · Presence · Direction

A portrait is not taken. It is directed, then allowed to be owned by the person standing in the light.

01First application

LG Studio used El Retrato to understand Sharnay Photography before deciding what to build.

02First read

Erika Sharnay's practice is portrait and editorial photography for models and aspiring models: directed presence, styling, and visual expression.

03What became visible

Creative vision, confidence, and the right to own expression in front of the camera. Stronger portraits followed from clearer direction, not from a louder effect.

04What direction made possible

With a clearer frame, Sharnay Photography could refine how it presents and directs an editorial portrait experience, and later carry that same rigor into every session.

Author's note

El Retrato is a pilot system developed by LG Studio to make visible the perspective behind a brand before deciding what to build.

It began with Sharnay Photography as the first project where it was put to the test: the idea that understanding who leads the practice, before the practice itself, transforms the result with greater rigor and honesty.
