The premise

It does not begin
with a solution.
It begins with a read.

A traditional brief describes the task. A read explains the person doing it. El Retrato collects your words, references, and images, and returns a clear starting point built from evidence.

Two ways to use it

01

Read your own practice.

Stop in front of your work and see it clearly before deciding what to build next.

02

Open one with a client.

Understand the commission before answering it. Lead from clarity, not assumption.

First application

Sharnay
Photography

The first read

LG Studio used El Retrato to understand Sharnay Photography before deciding what to build. Erika Sharnay’s practice is portrait and editorial photography for models and aspiring models: directed presence, styling, and visual expression.

What became visible

Creative vision, confidence, and the right to own expression in front of the camera. Stronger portraits followed from clearer direction, not from a louder effect.

What direction made possible

With a clearer frame, Sharnay Photography could refine how it presents and directs an editorial portrait experience, and later carry that same rigor into every session.

El Retrato is an LG Studio system.
Sharnay Photography is the first practice it was built to read.
