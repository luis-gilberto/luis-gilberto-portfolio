I am pleased to share that Costello Law Firm has expanded its litigation team with the addition of Noelle Chung and Wendy Harper.

Noelle and Wendy give the firm greater depth across those disciplines while preserving the focused, senior-level representation that has always defined the practice.

Noelle joins us after serving as an Assistant Attorney General for the State of Washington, where she represented state agencies in enforcement actions, administrative proceedings, and appeals. Before and after her public service, she practiced at law firms in California and Washington, handling commercial, intellectual property, and regulatory disputes. Her practice focuses on professionals, executives, business owners, and organizations facing investigations, enforcement proceedings, and licensing matters.

Wendy is a former prosecutor with the Seattle City Attorney’s Office and a former judicial clerk, with extensive trial experience defending individuals in criminal matters. She has also represented companies in employment, regulatory, and liability matters at national firms, and spent more than a decade with the Federal Civil Rights Clinic. Her practice spans criminal, civil, and regulatory defense, civil rights and employment matters, and internal investigations.

I remain lead counsel and set the strategy on the firm’s most significant matters. What has changed is the depth of the team around me: complementary experience in trial work, government enforcement, regulatory proceedings, and complex litigation, brought to bear on each matter from the first call.

Our approach has not changed. We prepare every matter as if it will be tried, and we pay early attention to the consequences a decision in one proceeding may create in another. We are also glad to work alongside existing counsel, as co-counsel or as lead, depending on what the matter requires. Your relationship with your client stays intact.

When a client of yours is facing a white collar investigation, a regulatory or professional licensing matter, a parallel proceeding, or a complex commercial dispute, I would welcome the call.
