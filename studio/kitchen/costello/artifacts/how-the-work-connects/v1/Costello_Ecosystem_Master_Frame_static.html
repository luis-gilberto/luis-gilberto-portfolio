Dear «FirstName»,

I am pleased to share that Costello Law Firm has grown, with the addition of Wendy Harper to our litigation team and Nari Weaver as the firm’s Chief of Staff.

Our work increasingly involves matters that cross several areas at once: white collar investigations, regulatory and professional licensing proceedings, parallel civil and criminal matters, and complex commercial disputes.

Wendy adds trial depth to that work. Nari brings the operational discipline a growing practice needs. Together, they add depth without changing the focused, senior-level representation that has always defined the firm.

Wendy is a former prosecutor with the Seattle City Attorney’s Office and a former judicial clerk, with extensive trial experience defending individuals in criminal matters. She has also represented companies in employment, regulatory, and liability matters at national firms, and spent more than a decade with the Federal Civil Rights Clinic. Her practice spans criminal, civil, and regulatory defense, civil rights and employment matters, and internal investigations.

Nari leads the firm’s operations and communications. She brings more than fifteen years across communications, program management, nonprofit leadership, and operations, and her role is to make sure the firm runs with the same discipline we bring to a matter: clear communication with clients and referring counsel, and a practice built to grow without losing focus.

I remain lead counsel and set the strategy on the firm’s most significant matters. What has changed is the depth of the team around me, both in the courtroom and in how the firm is run.

Our approach has not changed. We prepare every matter as if it will be tried, and we pay early attention to the consequences a decision in one proceeding may create in another. We are also glad to work alongside existing counsel, as co-counsel or as lead, depending on what the matter requires. Your relationship with your client stays intact.

When a client of yours is facing a white collar investigation, a regulatory or professional licensing matter, a parallel proceeding, or a complex commercial dispute, I would welcome the call.

Respectfully,
