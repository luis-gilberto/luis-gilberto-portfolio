Positioning anchor

One paragraph the website, bios and campaign materials all reinforce

Costello Law Firm defends individuals, professionals and business owners in high-stakes matters where a single set of allegations creates exposure on more than one front at once: criminal, civil, administrative and professional licensing. Decades of the firm's experience are concentrated in exactly that complexity, coordinating strategy across parallel proceedings so that what happens in one does not compromise another. Every matter is built for trial from day one, and that discipline is what produces results.

How it translates

Direct clientsEnter through the situation. A professional or business owner recognizes their own problem first, then discovers the breadth behind it.
Referring attorneysEnter through capability. The practice areas and the parallel-proceedings experience are visible immediately, in the language peers already use.
Campaign segmentsThe same anchor supports each segment in CLF's plan without rewriting the firm: white collar and small business now, then small law and industry-specific verticals as Q4 strategy is set.

Where it holds up

Health careA billing or fraud allegation can run as a criminal matter, a civil action and a licensing proceeding at the same time.
ConstructionOne dispute can surface contract, employment and regulatory exposure together, with the business and its owner both in scope.
Car dealersConsumer and industry-specific regulatory scrutiny can arrive alongside civil claims and, in some matters, criminal exposure.

Three industries that look nothing alike, all fitting the same pattern. The anchor does not need to change to reach any of them.

The three industries are the firm's own list. The example combinations illustrate the pattern rather than describe specific firm matters, and are open to correction.
