How the letters connect. The letters and the website share positioning, voice, approved credentials and claims, calls to action, and the firm story; the campaign consistency pass checks that relationship directly. Articles can supply themes for later letters, and article timing can inform when a later letter is released, without making any letter dependent on it. The firm owns the broader marketing plan; LG Studio owns the five-letter campaign defined in SOW-02. Credentials and claims confirmed for the website ledger carry into the letters, so the two stay consistent by design.

What "campaign complete" means

  • All five letters approved by the firm's point of contact
  • Letter 1 production and mailing handoff completed, or its remaining dependency documented
  • Factual and claim confirmations recorded for every letter
  • Campaign-wide consistency review complete
  • Production-ready files delivered for Letters 2 through 5 where production is authorized or applicable
  • Consolidated archive delivered: approved copy for all five, Letter 1 production file and approved proof, mailing-list production requirements, vendor specifications, campaign messaging references, production notes, and status record
  • Any outstanding third-party production dependency clearly documented

Scope boundary. This plan reflects LG / CLF / SOW-02. The fixed fee covers the five-letter campaign and the services listed in that agreement. Third-party printing, postage, mailing-house costs, stationery, and purchased lists sit outside the Studio fee. Anything beyond the defined Letter SOW requires separate written approval.
