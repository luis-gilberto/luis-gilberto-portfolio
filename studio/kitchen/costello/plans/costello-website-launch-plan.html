What we are launching

A complete implementation of costellolawfirm.com in firm-controlled infrastructure, built on the brand positioning, voice, approved copy, and page designs the firm already owns. The launch set is six pages:

1HomePrimary positioning, core situations, credibility signals, consultation path
2AboutFirm story, approach, and supporting credibility content
3AttorneysCurrent attorney presentation using approved bios, credentials, and supplied imagery
4Practice AreasOne overview page organizing approved practice areas and routes into consultation
5FAQsStructured questions using approved content and appropriate intake framing
6Contact / ConsultationInquiry form with delivery confirmation and required disclaimer treatment

Two rounds of revision per page are included within the review windows. Photography, additional pages, ongoing content, and legal review of website content sit outside this engagement; if the firm wants any of them, they are handled under a separate written agreement so this plan stays intact.

Kris gives affirmative written approval to launch. LG Studio handles the cutover and monitors the first 48 hours. The 30-day warranty on LG Studio's own work begins at launch.

How the three connect. The website and the referral-letter campaign are separate LG Studio engagements with their own scopes and schedules. The firm's bio, profile, article, and marketing work remains firm-owned. These internal initiatives do not independently gate the website schedule; approved materials available within the review windows can be integrated into the launch, and later outputs may inform website updates and future letter sequencing where relevant. The Attorneys page launches with bios in their current approved form, and rankings and recognitions on the site come from the confirmed ledger, so profile alignment and the website stay consistent by design.

What "ready to launch" means

  • Six-page launch set locked in writing and each page approved by the firm's approver
  • Claim-verification ledger confirmed in writing by the firm
  • Consultation form tested end to end, delivering to the right inbox with confirmation
  • Site verified on desktop, tablet, and phone across current browsers
  • Accessibility and performance baseline review complete, with any exceptions noted
  • Analytics and Search Console live in firm-owned accounts
  • Domain, DNS, hosting, source files, and credentials held in the firm's name
  • Affirmative written launch approval from Kris

After launch. LG Studio corrects defects in its own work at no charge for 30 days. The firm holds everything it needs to maintain, edit, or move the site without LG Studio, and LG Studio never holds the domain, hosting, or credentials in its own name.
