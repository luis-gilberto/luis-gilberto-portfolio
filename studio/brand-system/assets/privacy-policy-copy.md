## Privacy

Short version: this site does not track you across the web, does not use
advertising cookies, and does not sell anything about you to anyone.

The longer version is below, because a vague privacy policy is its own kind of
answer.

### What gets collected when you visit

This site uses Plausible Analytics. It is cookieless and does not collect
personal data. It records aggregate counts: pages viewed, referring site,
country, device type, browser. It does not build a profile of you, does not
follow you to other sites, and does not require a cookie banner, which is why
you have not seen one.

No advertising trackers run on this site. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel,
no LinkedIn Insight Tag.

Standard server logs are kept by the hosting provider for security and
performance. These include IP addresses and are retained on the provider's
schedule, not mine.

### What gets collected when you reach out

**If you book a conversation,** the scheduling flow collects your name, email,
and whatever you type into the notes field. It runs on Microsoft 365 Bookings.
That information is used to hold the meeting and follow up on it.

**If you email me,** I have your email and whatever you wrote. Same as any
email.

**If you subscribe to anything,** your email address is stored with the email
provider used to send it. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and it
works immediately.

That is the complete list. There is no lead-scoring pipeline behind it, no data
broker, no resale. Ever.

### Services this site relies on

- **Cloudflare Pages** — hosting and delivery
- **Plausible Analytics** — cookieless, privacy-first traffic measurement
- **Cloudinary** — image hosting and delivery
- **Microsoft 365** — email and meeting scheduling

Each of these sees only what it needs to do its job.

### How long things are kept

Analytics data is aggregate and holds nothing that identifies you. Emails and
booking records are kept as long as they are useful to an active or prospective
engagement, and cleared out when they are not.

### Your choices

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask me to delete it.
No specific legal jurisdiction required, and no form to fill out. Send an email
and I will do it.

If you would rather browse without being counted at all, any standard tracker
blocker will stop Plausible. Nothing on this site breaks if you do.

### Client work

Work performed for clients is governed by the agreement signed with that
client, not by this policy. Client materials, data, and anything shared during
an engagement stay confidential and are never used as training material,
promotional content, or a case study without written permission.
