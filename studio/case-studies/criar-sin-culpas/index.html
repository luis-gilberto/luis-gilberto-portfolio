CRIAR SIN CULPAS · LIVING CASE STUDY V1 · Evidence prototype

The trust was already there.
The system wasn't

Criar Sin Culpas began with something most brands spend years trying to earn: trust.

Parents were already listening. Nari had a recognizable voice, a growing audience, and years of expertise shaped by the questions families brought to her again and again.

The opportunity was to give that trust structure.

To turn a body of knowledge into something parents could navigate in the moments they needed it, return to over time, and eventually grow with.

What began as a brand and platform challenge became the architecture for something larger:

a system designed to carry trust from voice, to support, to product, to practice.

Voice→ Brand→ Platform→ Support→ Product→ Operations

03 The Read

Signals changed the read. The read changed what we built

Abundance without a read stays intuitive. StrategyIQ interpreted platform behavior · turning signal into the next design and product decision.

SIGNAL

What we observed

Parents arrived activated, scanning, and short on capacity · not browsing for leisure. Platform behavior showed urgency patterns, not editorial patience.

READ

What StrategyIQ suggested

Behavioral evidence over content assumptions · what families needed in the moment, not what a conventional site expects.

DECISION

What followed

The read shifted from designing for reading to designing for emotional urgency · hierarchy, protocols, navigation, and support architecture all followed.

04 What the signals changed
