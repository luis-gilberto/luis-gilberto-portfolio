CRIAR SIN CULPAS · LIVING CASE STUDY

The trust was already there.
The system wasn't

Criar Sin Culpas already had what many practices spend years trying to earn: trust, expertise, and an audience. But that value lived across disconnected formats and platforms. The engagement became the work of turning it into a support system parents could use and the practice could sustain.

Formation arc

Voice→ Brand→ Platform→ Support→ Product→ Operations

What began with voice became an owned system for support, learning, product, and operations.

05 Designing for the moment

Parents were not arriving to browse.
They were arriving in need.

They were exhausted, emotionally activated, and often using one hand while caring for a child. That changed hierarchy, writing, navigation, protocol length, illustration, and the choices offered next.

What the moment demanded

Exhausted Scanning Holding a child Emotionally activated Looking for one useful sentence

What the experience changed

  • Stronger hierarchy
  • Shorter intervention paths
  • Scannability
  • Protocols
  • Navigation
  • Writing
  • Illustration
  • Support-choice architecture

Scannability is a form of care.

06 From protocol to product signal

Each protocol supported the moment.
Each use taught us what came next.

Protocols were both interventions and product tests. They showed what parents reached for first, what language helped, and where immediate relief needed to become continued support.

Evidence / Product signal

The strongest value was not more content. It was practical relief, navigable language, and continuity.

Use → Observe → Learn → Change

Decision

Build a support ecosystem, not a content library.

Free protocols handle the moment. Deeper programs build capacity before the next one.

Free support → Evidence → Deeper support → New signals
