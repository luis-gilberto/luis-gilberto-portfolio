03 · What Sharnay should protect

Three commitments.

  1. Personal, not performative

    Sophistication should never create distance.

  2. Guided, not managed

    The client should feel accompanied.

  3. Received, not delivered

    The photos, the gallery, and the album should feel like the culmination of something.
