The Real Scope

Marketing rarely has a marketing-only problem.

An unclear offer, competing priorities, limited capacity, delayed decisions, or a disconnected customer experience can all appear to be marketing problems. That is why the work sometimes begins underneath the campaign, website, or content request.

LG Studio does not manage the client’s finances, operations, or executive team.

The Approach

Architecture first.
Surface second.

Work that feels designed rather than decorated, and calm rather than loud. Clarity, usability, consistency, and trust come before ornament.

The Practice Model

Senior involvement is the model.

The person reading the situation, setting direction, and staying accountable for the work is the senior person you hired.

LG Studio is intentionally small. I stay close to the decisions and remain accountable for the coherence of what gets made. When the work requires additional craft or production capacity, I bring in trusted collaborators while retaining strategic and creative direction.
