The Real Scope

Marketing rarely has a marketing-only problem.

An unclear offer, competing priorities, limited capacity, delayed decisions, or a disconnected customer experience can all appear to be marketing problems.

That is why the work sometimes begins underneath the campaign, website, or content request. I examine the offer, audience, priorities, decision environment, operating constraints, and existing marketing to understand what the strategy must account for.

I do not manage a client’s finances, run their operations, or replace their executive team. I read the business conditions marketing depends on, then help determine what marketing should do next.

What the direction can become.

The practice does not begin with a predetermined deliverable. It begins with the situation. Once the direction is clear, LG Studio builds the systems, experiences, and materials needed to carry it forward.

Strategy systems

Frameworks, diagnostics, client workspaces, and decision tools that turn scattered thinking into usable structure.

Editorial websites

Narrative-led web experiences that feel considered, human, and built to last.

Visual identity

Color, type, rhythm, and reference systems that stay consistent across everything a brand needs to say.

Campaign assets

The pieces that carry a launch: decks, pages, packets, scripts, and production references.

Interactive artifacts

Cinematic documents, guided experiences, and shareable moments that make the work feel real before it becomes a full product.

Client workspaces

Private rooms where a client’s work can live, stay organized, and move forward without chaos.

Asset libraries

A calm place where finished work, visual references, and production materials can be found and shared.

The Approach

Architecture first. Surface second.

The result is work that feels designed rather than decorated, and calm rather than loud. Clarity, usability, consistency, and trust come before ornament.

Latin warmth, Scandinavian restraint, and a belief that the smallest details are where a project earns its trust.

The Practice Model

Senior involvement is the model.

LG Studio is intentionally small. I lead the strategic work directly, stay close to the decisions, and remain accountable for the quality and coherence of what gets made.

When an engagement requires additional production capacity or specialized expertise, I bring in trusted collaborators while retaining strategic and creative direction.

The result is a practice with senior attention, flexible capability, and fewer layers between the problem and the person responsible for solving it.
