Pass-through at cost, with the vendor invoice attached and your approval before anything is ordered. A firm printer quote will be confirmed Friday, 4 September, before any commitment. Monday the 7th is Labor Day, so the vendor is briefed and the slot held ahead of the holiday rather than after it. Postage is fixed: a Forever stamp rose to 82¢ on 12 July.

Two recommendations

  • Real stamps, not a meter. Metered postage from one lawyer to another reads as a mass mailing, and this letter is the opposite of one. The difference across 500 pieces is about $20.
  • Split the list. The top fifty recipients get hand-addressed envelopes. The rest get printed. Everyone gets Kris’s signature in ink. The people most likely to send work get the version that visibly took effort.
If you want them out sooner
In the mail Friday 11 September

A one-day print turn moves signing to Thursday evening and drops the letters Friday. They land Monday the 14th through Wednesday the 16th. Rush printing adds roughly 25 to 50 percent to the print line only, not to the whole job.

One thing to decide
Before or after the LinkedIn announcement

On this schedule the letters land after the announcement, which makes each one the personal version of something the recipient may already have seen. If you would rather the letter arrive first, we move the print date up.
