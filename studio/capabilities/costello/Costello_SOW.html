01Purpose

Costello Law Firm has already invested in brand positioning, voice, messaging, approved website copy and initial page design. That work is substantially complete and, in the Studio's assessment, sound.

This engagement does not restart it. The Studio will take custody of the existing work, reconcile and complete it, build and launch the site, and transfer full ownership and control of every asset and account to the firm.

What this is not. This is not a rebranding engagement, a discovery process, or a retainer. It is a defined, fixed-fee project with a named end point and a documented handoff.

02Phase A · Website transition and launch

A1 · Secure. Collection and inventory of all existing assets:

  • Domain registrar access and DNS control
  • Existing website platform credentials and hosting
  • Design source files, brand assets and identity files
  • Final approved copy, content documents and imagery
  • Third-party licenses in effect (fonts, imagery, plugins, platforms)
  • Written inventory of what was received, what is missing, and what must be re-created

A2 · Stabilize. Reconciliation before any build:

  • Version reconciliation across copy, design and content documents
  • Claim-verification ledger covering years of experience, rankings, recognitions, memberships, teaching appointments, case counts and outcome statements, each with its source, for the firm's confirmation
  • Definition and written sign-off of the launch page set
  • Migration of the experience into firm-controlled infrastructure

A3 · Build and launch. Production of the launch set:

  • Six pages: Home, About, Attorneys, Practice Areas, FAQs, and Contact / Consultation
  • Responsive implementation across desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Design of pages not currently covered by existing comps, in the established visual system
  • Consultation request form with delivery and confirmation
  • Foundational technical SEO: titles, meta descriptions, headings, sitemap, schema, indexation
  • Analytics and search console configuration in firm-owned accounts
  • Accessibility and performance baseline review
  • Cross-browser and cross-device quality assurance
  • Launch, DNS cutover and post-launch verification
  • Written handoff: credentials, accounts, source files, licenses and administration guide
Revisions. Two rounds of revision per page are included, within the review windows described in section 05. Additional rounds are available and quoted before the work is done.

03Phase B · Referral letter campaign (optional, separate authorization)

Quoted separately so that it can be authorized, deferred or declined independently of Phase A.

  • Completion and finalization of the five referral letters
  • Announcement letter for the expanded team
  • Coordination with print and mailing vendors, including specification and proofing
  • Mailing list preparation support and send coordination
  • Alignment of letter content with the launched website
Third-party costs. Printing, stationery, postage, mailing house fees and any purchased lists are billed at cost with prior written approval, and are not included in the fee below.

04Not included

The following are outside this Scope of Work. Each can be quoted separately.

  • Additional pages beyond the six named in A3, including individual practice-area or situation pages
  • Ongoing content production, blogging or newsletter programs
  • Paid media, advertising placement or media buying
  • Photography, videography and illustration
  • Rebranding, new identity design or logo work
  • Case management, intake or CRM software selection and integration
  • Ongoing maintenance, hosting management or support after the warranty period in section 09
  • Legal review of website content or advertising compliance

05Client responsibilities

The schedule in section 06 assumes the following. Delay in any of them extends the schedule by the period of delay.

  • Access. Delivery of all items listed in A1, or written confirmation that an item cannot be obtained.
  • One approver. A single individual authorized to consolidate feedback and give binding approval.
  • Reviews within two business days. Written feedback on each deliverable within two business days of receipt.
  • Fact verification. Confirmation of the claim-verification ledger described in A2. Content is not published until the ledger is confirmed in writing.
  • Content and imagery. Any content, photography or documents the firm wishes to include that are not already in the existing package.
Professional responsibility. The firm is solely responsible for the accuracy of all statements about its lawyers, experience, results and recognitions, and for compliance with the Washington Rules of Professional Conduct governing lawyer advertising and communications. The Studio will assemble the verification ledger and flag anything that appears inconsistent, but does not provide legal or advertising-compliance advice.

08Ownership and handoff

On final payment for Phase A, all deliverables created under this agreement become the exclusive property of Costello Law Firm, PLLC, including copyright in the site design, layouts, code and written materials produced by the Studio.

In addition, at launch the firm will hold, in its own accounts and its own name:

  • The domain and DNS control
  • The website source project and all working files
  • Platform, CMS and form administrator credentials
  • Analytics and search console accounts
  • A documented register of every third-party license in use and its terms
  • A written handoff checklist confirming transfer of each of the above
No dependency. The Studio will not hold the domain, hosting, source files or credentials in its own name at any point after launch, and will not require the firm to retain the Studio in order to maintain, edit or move its website.

The Studio retains the right to describe the engagement and display the work in its portfolio, excluding any confidential firm or client information, and subject to the firm's prior written approval of what is shown.

09Acceptance, warranty and general terms

  • Acceptance. The launch set is deemed accepted on written approval to publish, or five business days after delivery for final review if no written response is received.
  • Warranty. For 30 days following launch, the Studio will correct defects in its own work at no charge. This covers functional and display defects, not new features, content changes or third-party platform changes.
  • Confidentiality. Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential and use it only for this engagement. The Studio's obligations extend to all firm and client information encountered during the work.
  • Independent contractor. The Studio is an independent contractor. Nothing here creates an employment, partnership or agency relationship.
  • Subcontractors. The Studio may engage subcontractors, remains responsible for their work, and will bind them to equivalent confidentiality obligations. Any related-party engagement will be disclosed in writing in advance.
  • Termination. Either party may terminate on seven days' written notice. On termination, the firm pays for work performed to that date, and the Studio delivers all work product, assets, credentials and source files completed to that point.
  • Limitation of liability. Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid under it. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.
  • Governing law. Washington State. Venue in King County.
  • Entire agreement. This document is the complete agreement between the parties on this subject and supersedes prior discussions. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties.
