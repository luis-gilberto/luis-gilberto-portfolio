            <div class="reading-content" id="intro">
                <p class="lead"><strong>In a year when "together" felt more precious than ever, Microsoft needed to position Family Safety as the go-to tool for modern parents.</strong></p>
                <p>We needed to show that peace of mind can be simple, intuitive, and deeply human. The challenge wasn't just launching another app; it was creating a conversation starter rather than a surveillance tool.</p>
                
                <div class="inset-card" style="border-left-color: #008272;">
                    <h4 style="color: #008272;">The Goal</h4>
                    <p>Fold technology into the family routine. Our guiding light: design and build with the goal of bringing families together, in the best and most chaotic times.</p>
                </div>
            </div>

            <!-- 2. STRATEGY -->
            <div class="reading-content" id="strategy">
                <h3>Strategic Approach</h3>
                <p>As Campaign Strategy & Creative Direction lead, I orchestrated cross-functional teams across Microsoft's product, marketing, and creative divisions. Our goal: develop a globally resonant campaign that balanced technological innovation with authentic family dynamics.</p>
                <p>We built a <strong>Modular Campaign Architecture</strong>: a scalable Bill of Materials spanning digital, social, and in-app touchpoints. This enabled seamless global deployment while maintaining consistent family-first messaging.</p>
            </div>

            <!-- 3. RESULTS -->
            <div class="reading-content" id="results">
                <h3>Impact & Results</h3>
                <p>Within weeks of launch, tens of thousands of families adopted the app and shared heartfelt stories of feeling safer and more connected. Social buzz spiked, and tech press praised our human-centric hero film—proof that narrative-driven marketing builds real emotional resonance.</p>

                <p>This campaign reinforced that when launching a tool as personal as family safety, the narrative must lead with empathy and empowerment, not features and functions. By centering authentic family moments, we created a foundation for sustained adoption and genuine emotional connection.</p>
