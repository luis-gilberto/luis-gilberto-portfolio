===============================
3) Notes for Trae
===============================
- Keep the section id as "services" so existing internal links continue to work.
- This is an advisory‑first offering; there is no "Operations/Analytics" pillar here to avoid overpromising. If those topics appear elsewhere, link to the Toolkit instead of listing them as services.
- Icons: .cs-card-icon is a slot. You can drop in your existing SVGs inside that div or keep the soft coral square.
- Typography pulls Playfair (titles) and Inter (UI/body). Ensure weights 500/600 for Playfair and 400/600 for Inter are loaded; otherwise the browser will fake bold.
- Mobile: grid collapses to 1 column under 920px; spacing remains compact to reduce page length.
- Colors use your brand coral/teal; adjust CSS variables if your tokens differ.
