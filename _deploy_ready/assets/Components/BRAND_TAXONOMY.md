# Luis Gilberto Brand Taxonomy
## Official Source of Truth

**Last Updated:** November 2, 2025  
**Purpose:** Eliminate confusion between product offering and editorial platform

---

## 🎯 Site Architecture

| Section | Official Name | URL | Purpose | Type |
|---------|---------------|-----|---------|------|
| **Product Offering** | **The Hub** | `/TheHub` | Productized frameworks, systems, and repeatable processes. Where years of experience become scalable tools. | Service/Product |
| **Editorial Platform** | **Insights** | `/insights` | Thought leadership, case studies, reflections, monthly deep dives on marketing strategy and creative leadership. | Editorial/Blog |
| **Main Portfolio** | Luis Gilberto | `/` | Professional identity, gateway to both offerings | Portfolio/Home |

---

## ✅ Terminology Rules

### **DO say:**
- "The Hub" when referring to the productized service offering
- "Insights" when referring to the editorial blog/content platform
- "luis-gilberto.com/TheHub" for the product URL
- "luis-gilberto.com/insights" for the editorial URL

### **DON'T say:**
- ❌ "Insights Hub" (creates confusion)
- ❌ "The Hub blog" (The Hub is not a blog)
- ❌ "Hub insights" (ambiguous)
- ❌ Just "Hub" without "The" (lacks brand precision)

---

## 📝 Messaging Differentiation

### **The Hub (Product)**
**Positioning:** Productized experience  
**Audience:** Clients seeking frameworks and systems  
**Value Prop:** "Translating years of intuition into scalable, actionable tools"  
**Tone:** Transformative, ROI-focused, solution-oriented  
**Content Type:** Frameworks, templates, processes, consulting services

### **Insights (Editorial)**
**Positioning:** Thought leadership platform  
**Audience:** Marketing professionals, creative leaders, industry peers  
**Value Prop:** "Real thinking from the trenches—no corporate jargon, no clickbait"  
**Tone:** Authentic, editorial, reflective, strategic  
**Content Type:** Case studies, post-mortems, personal reflections, monthly articles

---

## 🔗 Cross-References

When mentioning one in the context of the other:

- **From The Hub → Insights:**  
  "Want to see these frameworks in action? Check out my monthly Insights where I break down real-world applications."

- **From Insights → The Hub:**  
  "Ready to implement these strategies yourself? Explore The Hub for productized frameworks and tools."

---

## 🚨 Common Confusion Points (Resolved)

| Confusion | Clarification |
|-----------|---------------|
| "Is Insights part of The Hub?" | **No.** They are separate offerings. Insights is editorial content; The Hub is a productized service. |
| "What's the difference?" | **The Hub = What you buy. Insights = What you read.** |
| "Where do I find case studies?" | **Insights** (editorial platform). The Hub contains frameworks, not case studies. |
| "Where do I buy your frameworks?" | **The Hub** (product offering). Insights showcases thinking, not products. |

---

## 📋 Reference Checklist

When creating new content, ask:

1. **Is this about The Hub (product) or Insights (editorial)?**
2. **Am I using the correct URL?**
3. **Have I used the right terminology throughout?**
4. **Would this confuse someone unfamiliar with the site structure?**
