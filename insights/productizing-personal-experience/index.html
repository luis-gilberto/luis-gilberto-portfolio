Productizing Personal Experience

By Luis Gilberto · October 2025 · 6 min read
TL;DR: A placeholder article that sketches how to turn hard‑won lessons into practical products — frameworks, tools, and stories that help others move faster with clarity.

Real transformations often begin as personal experiments. This article explores a simple approach: capture your process, distill what’s repeatable, and design artifacts that help someone else get to a useful outcome — faster, with less friction.

Capture What Works

Start with what you actually do. Write down each step, the decisions you make, and the cues you look for. Keep it lightweight. The goal isn’t documentation for its own sake — it’s creating leverage you can reuse.

Distill to a Clear Path

From raw notes, define the minimum viable path: inputs, steps, and outcomes. Turn this into a one‑page framework or checklist that someone else can follow with confidence.

Design for Use

Make it practical: name it, package it, and give it a crisp introduction. A good product starts where the user is and ends at a clear outcome, with just enough guidance in between.
