This plate makes one claim: the four-part logic was already present in Nari Fateha's teaching before anyone named it. It did not cause the engagement's strategic turn, which came a week earlier and for a different reason. The language below was in use from 2023, spread across workshops, live classes, audio lessons and social posts, never presented as a model and never gathered in one place. Where an original file no longer exists, the fragment is set here as an editorial reconstruction of her wording, reviewed and approved by her before publication. Underlines are the studio's, added to mark what recurred.
