Emotional Positioning

In a year when "together" felt more precious than ever, Microsoft needed to reframe digital safety... not as surveillance, but as connection. The Family Safety app wasn’t just a parental tool. It was a wellness companion. One that respected autonomy while reinforcing trust.

The Narrative Shift

I saw an opportunity to shift the narrative. Not through features, but through feeling. We didn’t lead with control; we led with clarity. We built a story that was warm, real, and instantly graspable. One that made parents feel empowered, not anxious.

The pivot was subtle but profound: safety wasn’t about knowing where your kids were. It was about knowing they felt safe enough to reach out.

Motion & Humanity

That shift changed everything. It shaped the hero film, the onboarding flow, and the way we spoke to families. It gave the product motion and humanity.

Families told us they traded "Where are you?" texts for a collective exhale. That was the win. Not the metrics. Not the downloads. The feeling.

This is how we earned trust during a global crisis.
